## Production Notes

- Keep text overlays to one short line where possible; Apple Watch screenshots are small.
- Maintain the same warm brown/yellow palette as the app UI, but use stronger contrast for captions.
- Avoid settings, onboarding, or generic app icon-only shots.
- For final assets, generate one localized set per locale. Do not reuse English captions in `zh-Hans`.
