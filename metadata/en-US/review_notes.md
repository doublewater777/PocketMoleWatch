# Review Notes

## App Access
No login required. The app launches directly into the game on Apple Watch.

## Testing
- Launch the app on a paired Apple Watch or watchOS Simulator
- Tap "Play" to start a 30-second round
- Tap the moles as they appear; avoid bombs (red)
- Golden moles are worth double points
- Game ends after 30 seconds with a result screen

## Notable Features
- Offline-only: no network calls, no account required
- Haptic feedback and sound effects for gameplay
- Local-only best score tracking (no data transmission)
- Watch-only app (no iPhone companion app)
