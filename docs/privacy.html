Pocket Mole Watch

Privacy Policy

Pocket Mole Watch is designed to be a lightweight Apple Watch game that respects user privacy.

Last updated: May 5, 2026

Information Collection

Pocket Mole Watch does not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The app does not require account creation, sign-in, or third-party advertising SDKs.

Local Device Data

The app may store simple gameplay data on your device, such as your best score, so progress can persist between sessions. This information remains on your device unless it becomes part of your own Apple backup.

Tracking

Pocket Mole Watch does not track users across apps or websites and does not sell user data.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to This Policy

If privacy practices change in a future release, this page will be updated before those changes take effect.

Contact
